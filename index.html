Due date is calculated from the issue date and return period. You can adjust it if needed.
Circulation Guidelines
  • Each student can hold a maximum of 4 books simultaneously.
  • Choose a 7-day or 14-day return period; the due date is calculated automatically.
  • Late penalty accrues at ₹5.00 per day past the designated return deadline.
  • Choose WhatsApp or Email on the issue receipt to prepare the book issue message.
  • Upon issuing, an official thermal receipt slip is generated with transaction details.
